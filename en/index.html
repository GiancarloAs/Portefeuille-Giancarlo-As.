About me

A multidisciplinary designer combining visual design, motion design, brand strategy and AI integrated into the creative process.

Two profiles: creativity and business sense

Trained in graphic design in Peru (3-year diploma) and holder of a BTS in International Trade from Neuilly-sur-Seine, where I lived for 6 years. I’m fluent in French and at home in French business culture.

An AI-enhanced workflow

I use generative AI (prompt engineering, image and animated video generation) in my workflow to speed up production, with results that look polished, realistic and professional rather than artificial.
